Core System Capabilities

Multilingual Voice Intake

Caregivers speak naturally in Telugu, Hindi, or English. Our on-device NLU tokenizes child names, age, anthropometrics, parents' names, and ward without touching a keyboard.

WHO Growth Radar

Instant Z-score calculation for Stunting (HAZ), Underweight (WAZ), and Wasting (WHZ) with clear visual color bands, replacing manual printed charts.

Empathetic Parent Counseling

Translates intimidating clinical percentiles into warm, encouraging dialogues in regional languages, recommending affordable kitchen foods (groundnut, jaggery, drumstick leaves).

Connect the Dots Synthesis

Fuses longitudinal multi-point records across 30 to 90 days to discover the root cause behind weight stalls before acute malnutrition (SAM) sets in.

100% Offline SQLite

Complete Room database operating with zero latency in remote rural hamlets where 3G/4G connectivity is unavailable.

Zero-Trust Local Privacy

Sensitive biometric health records remain stored securely on the local device, never exposed to open public cloud registries.
